Welcome Home

A family chasing after good news together

Good News Orange County Church exists to share the hope and love of Jesus with our city. Whether you've been walking with God for decades or you're just curious, you'll find genuine community and a place to grow.

We gather to worship, to learn, and to do life together — across every age, background, and stage of the journey. No perfect people required.
